# voice.md

Starter profile: a calm, soothing voice. Not based on real transcripts yet. Replace sections as the
real voice becomes clear.

## Who I am talking to

One person, winding down. They clicked because they want to feel calmer and learn something
without being shouted at. They may be watching at the end of the day, maybe with the sound low.

## How I actually talk

- Slow and unhurried, roughly 120-130 words per minute, not 150. Leave room for pauses.
- Short, gentle sentences. One thought at a time.
- Warm and direct: "you", never "you guys".
- Invites, never commands: "Let's take a look", "Notice how...", "You might find..."
- Pauses are written into the script as `(pause)`. Silence is allowed.
- Calm curiosity instead of hype. The question is opened quietly, not with a jolt.

## Words I never use

insane, crazy, mind-blowing, game-changer, unlock, smash that like button, you won't believe,
hacks, let's dive in, let's get into it, guys, SHOCKING, anything in all caps.

## Words I do use

"Let's slow down for a moment", "notice", "take a breath", "gently", "here's the quiet part",
"there's no rush".

## What I will not claim

No numbers, studies or results I cannot show. No health or medical promises ("this cures stress").
No fake urgency.

## My format

- No channel intro, no subscribe pitch at the start.
- Opens softly but with a clear question in the first 15 seconds.
- Visuals: calm, slow shots, soft natural sound. No fast cuts, no jump-cut zooms, no sound effects.
- One gentle ask at the end, said once.
- No swearing.
