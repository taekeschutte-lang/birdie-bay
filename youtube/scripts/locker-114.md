# Locker 114: narrated story script

Voice: calm, soothing narrator. `(pause)` = one slow breath.

## HOOK (0:00-0:15)

[ON SCREEN: Slow push-in on a dented school locker, number 114. Hallway noise, muffled.]

You knew a kid like Sam. Locker 114, eyes down, every day. (pause)
Two years later his bullies would not stop asking how. (pause)
Almost nobody knows why.

## THE TURN (0:15-0:45)

[ON SCREEN: Title card, white text on black: "Locker 114". Then a boy, seen from behind, walking alone down a long hallway.]

So let's go back, slowly, to the night it changed. (pause)
Because it wasn't the money. And it wasn't the muscles. It was something much smaller.

## BEAT 1: The hallway

[ON SCREEN: Backpack hitting the floor. Laughter out of focus. Sam picking up his books, alone.]

Every morning was the same. A shove. A laugh. A name he never chose.
Sam learned to walk close to the wall. He learned to be early, so the hallway was empty.
(pause)
And he learned something worse. He started to believe them.

## BEAT 2: The night

[ON SCREEN: Dark bedroom. Only the glow of a phone on Sam's face. Rain on the window.]

One night, lying awake, Sam asked himself a quiet question.
What if I stop waiting for them to change their minds about me?
(pause)
He didn't have a plan. He just had that question. And it was enough to get up.

## BEAT 3: The gym

[ON SCREEN: Early morning, empty gym. Sam lifting a very light bar. Shaking arms. Close-up of a handwritten notebook: dates, small numbers.]

The first week, he could barely lift the empty bar.
Someone laughed there too. (pause) He came back the next day anyway.
He wrote everything down in a small notebook. Not to impress anyone. Just to see that yesterday's Sam was a little weaker than today's.

## BEAT 4: The first store

[ON SCREEN: Laptop at night, a simple online store on screen. A notification-less screen. Sam's tired face.]

At night, he taught himself how online stores work. Dropshipping.
His first store sold nothing. (pause) Not one order.
The second one didn't either.
So he did what he did at the gym. He changed one small thing. And tried again.

## BEAT 5: Slowly, then all at once

[ON SCREEN: Montage, gentle music. Notebook pages filling up. The bar getting heavier. The first order notification. Sam smiling, alone, quietly.]

Months passed. Nobody at school noticed.
Then one evening, his phone lit up. (pause) An order.
Then another.
His shirts started to fit differently. He stopped walking close to the wall.

## BEAT 6: They notice

[ON SCREEN: The same hallway, two years later. Sam walking in the middle. The old bullies by the lockers, watching. Their smiles fading.]

Two years after that night, Sam walked down the same hallway.
Same lockers. Same boys.
But this time, nobody laughed. (pause)
They watched. And on their faces was something Sam had never seen there before.
Jealousy.

## THE PAYOFF

[ON SCREEN: One of the bullies stepping toward Sam. "How did you do it?" in quiet subtitles. Sam stopping at locker 114.]

And then one of them asked him. "How did you do it?"
(pause)
This is the answer. The why almost nobody knows.
Sam said: "I stopped waiting for you to believe in me."
(pause)
That was it. Not the store. Not the gym. One decision, on one quiet night.
And that is what they could never copy.

## THE CLOSE

[ON SCREEN: Sam closing locker 114 for the last time and walking out into soft daylight. Fade to black.]

If someone ever made you walk close to the wall, (pause) maybe tonight is your quiet night.
Take a breath. (pause) And start small.
